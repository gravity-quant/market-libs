<objective>
Fan the frozen parity helper out to the remaining five packages and close the phase against
criterion 5.

The helper, its single metric, its four-rule normalization table and its per-package integer
floors were all settled in Plan 32-04 and proved against market-data — the package that actually
had a defect. This plan is deliberately the cheap half: five thin delegating files, each three
assertions, so the walker exists once and not six times. Six copies of the walker would recreate
exactly the drift that `tools/check_decode_intactness.py` exists to prevent.

The one place this plan is not mechanical is wallets. It is the single pre-Phase-7 package: no
`Client`, no `AsyncClient`, module-level request functions, and a public module surface of
exactly one name on the sync side. Its floor of 1 asserts almost nothing, and the tempting move —
an early return on the class axis — would make it pass vacuously. So wallets asserts its own
absence instead, and the floor is documented as near-vacuous by construction rather than hidden
behind a number that looks robust.

Purpose: complete criterion 3's coverage and prove criterion 5.
Output: five parity tests and a full local CI reproduction.
</objective>
