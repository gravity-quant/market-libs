<objective>
Bring `main_iol.py` and `main_ambito_financiero.py` onto the Plan 01 mechanism. Unlike higyrus and
matriz, these two already **survive** strict mode — they just classify a decode divergence wrong.

Purpose: CONTEXT D-05 says no driver catches `<Pkg>DecodeError` today; that is accurate for higyrus
and matriz only. `main_iol.py` has 12 broad-Exception handlers and `main_ambito_financiero.py` has 6,
so the decode error is caught **inside** the probe body before it can ever reach a decorator, and is
minted as an `ERROR-MAP` finding whose title is unique per probe — which `idempotent_by_title` cannot
dedupe, so every run pollutes the census with fresh fids. The fix is therefore a per-probe
`IOLDecodeError` branch placed ahead of the broad handler, not a decorator interception. The
decorator is still applied to all 22 probes because the endpoint/surface binding (D-02) is what makes
a finding triageable, and because 33-05's AST coverage gate requires it on all 130 probes.

Output: iol's 15 probes decorated with a `SHAPE`-classifying decode branch ahead of each broad
handler, ambito's 7 probes decorated with its missing fid seed, and both drivers threading the
two-pass strict flag through their single ctor sites.
</objective>
