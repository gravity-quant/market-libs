<objective>
Correct the envelope-unwrap bug in matriz's two Risk response parsers (D-03) so that the four
fields Plans 37-02/37-03 are about to type decode from the RIGHT nesting level. Typing without
this fix produces "campos tipados pero inertes" — annotations that can never populate.

Purpose: `report`, `detailedAccountReports` and `portfolio` live inside the vendor's
`detailedPosition` / `accountData` envelope. Today both parsers hand the ROOT body to `from_api`,
so `status` decodes as an extra key and not one declared field is ever found. This is the
precondition for the whole typing half of the phase (RESEARCH Pitfall 1).

Output: `_core.py` Risk parsers routed through `parse_envelope_response` + `unwrap`, five
falsified docstring claims corrected, and `test_core.py` carrying both the enveloped happy path
and an explicit lock on the flat-body disposition.
</objective>
