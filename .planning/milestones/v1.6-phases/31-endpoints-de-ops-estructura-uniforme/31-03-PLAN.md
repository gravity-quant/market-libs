<objective>
**The tracer slice.** One endpoint, one field, every layer — declared, decoded, dispatched on
both surfaces, re-exported, golden-regenerated, and strict-typechecked by CI. Verified end to
end BEFORE the pattern is expanded to market-data's eight models in plans 31-04 and 31-05.

`higyrus.get_health` is the right tracer for three reasons: it is the smallest possible model
(one `str` field), it is the only one of the five endpoints whose package is enrolled in mypy
`files` (so CI actually typechecks the result — market-data is not, D-13), and it is the only
one whose public surface is covered by the cross-package golden in
`verification/snapshots/`, which CI never runs and which therefore needs a deliberate,
locally-verified regeneration step (G-1).

Everything that plans 31-04 and 31-05 will repeat at larger scale is exercised here once, at
production quality, at the smallest possible blast radius: `SafeModel.to_dict()` copied
verbatim per package, a parser gaining `@_decode._response_parser` while keeping its guard,
four signature sites moved, a re-export, a golden regen, and the re-mock discipline that fixes
the TEST rather than the guard.

Purpose: if any of those seven mechanics is wrong, it is far cheaper to discover it on a
one-field model than on a four-level-deep health-feed tree.

Output: `higyrus_client.Health`, a typed `get_health` on both surfaces, and a regenerated
surface golden.
</objective>
