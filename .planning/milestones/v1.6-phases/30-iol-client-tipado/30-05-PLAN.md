<objective>
Close CR-02: `parse_get_instruments_by_type_response` iterates an unvalidated
`titulos` value. Phase 30 rewrote it from an inert `return titulos` into an active
model-building loop without adding the shape discipline its two sibling parsers
gained in the same commit family.

a string `"GGAL"` under `titulos` yields 4 all-default `Titulo` rows; a 2-key dict

has no attribute 'get'` that escapes the `IOLClientError` hierarchy every documented
caller catches.

synthetic financial rows. This is the same "silent degradation masks a changed or
compromised upstream" failure mode `_parse_list_or_raise` was hardened against in
this same phase (`_core.py` docstring, D-06 / ASVS V5) — CR-02 is the one parser
that got the rewrite without the guard.

Output: two `isinstance` guards in `_core.py`, five tests in `test_core.py`, one
async-surface parity test in `test_async_client.py`.
