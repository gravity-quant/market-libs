Fan out to the two packages that have no models module yet: `iol-client` and
`ambito-financiero-client`.

The copy topology is five packages, not three. iol receives its models module in
Phase 30 and must find a working decoder already in place rather than
bootstrapping one mid-phase; ambito's surface returns a scalar today and the copy
is dormant there by design. Both still need the mode carrier, the filter fix and
the sentinel, because the intactness gate and the per-package sentinel criteria
are read as five copies plus one documented exemption.

the strongest available evidence that the module has no hidden coupling — which
is what makes the verbatim-copy contract enforceable at all.
Output: two `_decode.py` copies, two decode errors, the mode carrier on eight
public entry points, two filter fixes, four test files touched, two snapshots
regenerated.

Decisions implemented: D-02 (the copy topology is five packages, with the sixth
carrying a documented exemption recorded in Plan 09), D-03 (flag on
`_ClientState`, ContextVar bound at the top of both `_request` methods with no
reset), D-05 (filter fix plus a caplog sentinel per package).
</objective>
