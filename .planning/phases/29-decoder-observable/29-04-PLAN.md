The benchmark has exactly three arms and reporting fewer than three is a failed
spike:
- **Arm A — status quo reference.** Today's `from_api` with uncached type-hint
  resolution. This is the reference, NOT the comparator.
- **Arm B — the honest comparator.** The shipped hints-cached walker: import
  `hints_for` and `walk_model` from `higyrus_client._decode` and time a full
  model decode through them. Because every module in this repo carries the
  future-annotations import, annotations are strings that must be re-evaluated
  on each uncached resolution; the per-class cache is what removes that cost, and
  it is already shipped as part of this phase's scope. Benchmarking msgspec
  against arm A rather than arm B is how the spike produces a decision the
  evidence does not support.
- **Arm C — msgspec.** `msgspec.convert` against the same shapes.

Payload/shape set, all three arms over the same shapes: a higyrus 21-field model
and a 22-field model (frozen with slots); a market-data 8-field model; and at
least one matriz-shaped dataclass that is frozen with NO slots. The last one is
mandatory — the only prior msgspec evidence in this project covered the
frozen-plus-slots shape, so a decision signed without it would not cover 18 of
matriz's dataclasses.

Also record, as capability evidence rather than timing evidence, what msgspec
cannot do: converting a payload carrying keys the target does not declare, and
converting a payload with several simultaneous problems. Report what msgspec
does in each case. This is the substance of why the walker is primary in both
branches.
