operator dispositions in `17-VALIDATION.md`, mirroring the Phase 11 LIVE-01 (`11-VALIDATION.md`)
operator-gated structure. This plan is OPERATOR-DRIVEN (`autonomous: false`, D-01): the operator

land a regression test this phase; EXPECTED/NO-FIX are non-gating).

ERG-01 with_options) introduced no observable wire regressions against the live APIs.

carrying any new operator dispositions; cycle-closure markers consistent with `verify_cycle_closure`.
</objective>
