new RED fixtures in the iol test file.

future contributor with no gate to stop them. The discriminator was implemented and run over
all six packages during research: it reddens exactly 2 sites (the two this phase fixes) and
spares all 10 matriz `Literal | None` leaves, so the ratchet lands with zero collateral.

tests, and a non-vacuity floor on the real-tree green test.
